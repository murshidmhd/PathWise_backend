Career: Civil Engineer
RIASEC Type: Realistic (R)
Description: Civil Engineers design and oversee construction of infrastructure like roads, bridges, buildings, dams and water systems.
Skills Required: AutoCAD, Structural Analysis, Construction Management, Surveying, Project Management
Salary India: Entry level 3-5 LPA, Mid level 7-15 LPA, Senior 18-35 LPA, Gulf 25-60 LPA
How to Start: 12th PCM → B.Tech Civil → Site engineer → Project engineer → Senior roles
Relevant for Kerala Students: Kerala infrastructure boom, Smart City Kochi projects, Gulf countries huge demand especially UAE and Qatar for Kerala civil engineers
