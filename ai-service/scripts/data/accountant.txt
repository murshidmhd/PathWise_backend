Career: Accountant / CA
RIASEC Type: Conventional (C)
Description: Accountants manage financial records, taxation, auditing and financial planning for individuals and organizations.
Skills Required: Tally, Excel, GST knowledge, Income Tax, Financial Reporting, Auditing
Salary India: B.Com fresher 2-4 LPA, CA fresher 6-10 LPA, Senior CA 20-60 LPA, Big 4 firms 8-20 LPA
How to Start: B.Com or BBA → CA Foundation → CA Intermediate → CA Final → Articleship → Practice
Relevant for Kerala Students: Every business needs accountants, Gulf accounting jobs pay 3-4x India salary, Big 4 firms have Kochi offices
