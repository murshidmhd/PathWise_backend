Career: Entrepreneur
RIASEC Type: Enterprising (E)
Description: Entrepreneurs start and run their own businesses. Can be in any sector from tech startups to traditional business to social enterprise.
Skills Required: Business Planning, Leadership, Sales, Financial Management, Risk Taking, Networking
Salary India: Highly variable, losses possible early, successful founders earn crores
How to Start: Identify a problem → Validate idea → Build MVP → Find customers → Funding if needed → Scale
Relevant for Kerala Students: Kerala Startup Mission KSUM provides funding and mentoring, Kochi startup ecosystem growing, tourism and food tech strong sectors
