Career: Writer / Content Writer
RIASEC Type: Artistic (A)
Description: Writers create content for websites, blogs, social media, books, scripts, journalism and marketing. Highly flexible career with remote options.
Skills Required: Strong Language Skills, SEO, Research, Storytelling, Content Strategy, Social Media
Salary India: Entry level 2-5 LPA, Experienced 8-20 LPA, Freelance writers earn 50k-3L per month
How to Start: Start a blog → Guest posts → Freelance on Upwork → Content agencies → Full time roles
Relevant for Kerala Students: Malayalam content market growing fast, English content writing fully remote, film script writing emerging in Kerala
