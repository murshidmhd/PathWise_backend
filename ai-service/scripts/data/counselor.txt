Career: Counselor / Career Counselor
RIASEC Type: Social (S)
Description: Counselors guide individuals through personal, academic or career challenges. School counselors, career counselors and therapists fall here.
Skills Required: Active Listening, Empathy, Communication, Psychology basics, Assessment Tools
Salary India: School counselor 3-6 LPA, Career counselor 4-10 LPA, Corporate counselor 8-20 LPA
How to Start: BA Psychology or Social Work → Certification in counseling → School or NGO → Private practice
Relevant for Kerala Students: School counselor positions in every Kerala school now mandatory, Gulf NRI family counseling growing
