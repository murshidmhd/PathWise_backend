Career: Psychologist
RIASEC Type: Social (S), Investigative (I)
Description: Psychologists study human behavior and help people with mental health, career counseling, organizational behavior and research.
Skills Required: Counseling, Empathy, Research Methods, Communication, Assessment Tools
Salary India: Entry level 3-6 LPA, Clinical psychologist 8-20 LPA, Corporate psychologist 10-25 LPA
How to Start: BA Psychology → MA Psychology → MPhil or PhD for clinical → RCI license → Practice
Relevant for Kerala Students: Mental health awareness growing in Kerala, school counselor demand high, corporate wellness programs expanding
