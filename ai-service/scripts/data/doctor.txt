Career: Doctor (Medical)
RIASEC Type: Investigative (I), Social (S)
Description: Doctors diagnose and treat illnesses, injuries, and medical conditions. They work in hospitals, clinics, or private practice.
Skills Required: Biology, Chemistry, Patient Communication, Critical Thinking, Anatomy, Pharmacology
Salary India: MBBS fresher 3-8 LPA govt, Specialist 15-50 LPA, Private practice highly variable
How to Start: Score well in 12th Science → NEET → MBBS 5.5 years → MD/MS specialization optional
Relevant for Kerala Students: Kerala has high doctor density. Government medical colleges in Kochi, Kozhikode, Trivandrum. Gulf opportunities very high for Kerala doctors.
