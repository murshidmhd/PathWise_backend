Career: Animator
RIASEC Type: Artistic (A)
Description: Animators create motion graphics, 2D or 3D animations for films, games, advertising, YouTube and social media.
Skills Required: Adobe After Effects, Blender, Maya, Photoshop, Storytelling, Motion Graphics
Salary India: Entry level 2-5 LPA, Mid level 6-18 LPA, Game industry 10-30 LPA
How to Start: Learn After Effects or Blender → Build showreel → YouTube channel → Studio or freelance
Relevant for Kerala Students: Malayalam YouTube boom creating huge animation demand, Kochi film industry, remote work common
