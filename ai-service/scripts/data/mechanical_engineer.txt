Career: Mechanical Engineer
RIASEC Type: Realistic (R)
Description: Mechanical Engineers design, build and maintain mechanical systems and machines. Works in manufacturing, automotive, aerospace, energy sectors.
Skills Required: CAD software, Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Manufacturing Processes, AutoCAD, SolidWorks
Salary India: Entry level 3-6 LPA, Mid level 8-18 LPA, Senior 20-40 LPA
Top Companies India: ISRO, BHEL, Tata Motors, L&T, Mahindra, KELTRON Kerala
How to Start: 12th PCM → B.Tech Mechanical → Gate exam for PSU → or private sector jobs
Relevant for Kerala Students: FACT Kochi, BPCL Kochi Refinery, KSEB, Naval Base Ezhimala hire mechanical engineers
