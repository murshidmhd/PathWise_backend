Career: Graphic Designer
RIASEC Type: Artistic (A)
Description: Graphic Designers create visual content for brands, marketing, digital media and print. Work in agencies, companies or freelance.
Skills Required: Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma, Typography, Color Theory, UI/UX basics, Branding
Salary India: Entry level 2-5 LPA, Mid level 6-15 LPA, Freelance income highly variable
How to Start: Learn Photoshop and Illustrator → Build portfolio → Freelance on Fiverr → Agency jobs
Relevant for Kerala Students: Huge freelance market, Kochi has growing design agencies, social media content demand very high
