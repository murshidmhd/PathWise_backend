Career: Research Analyst
RIASEC Type: Investigative (I)
Description: Research Analysts collect and interpret data to help organizations make strategic decisions. Works in market research, finance, consulting, or policy.
Skills Required: Data Analysis, Excel, Python or R, Report Writing, Critical Thinking, Statistics
Salary India: Entry level 3-6 LPA, Mid level 8-18 LPA, Senior 20-40 LPA
Top Companies India: KPMG, Deloitte, McKinsey, Nielsen, CRISIL, RBI
How to Start: Any bachelor's degree → MBA or MA Economics → Internships → Entry level analyst role
Relevant for Kerala Students: GIFT City remote roles, Kochi finance sector growing, SEBI registered firms
