Career: Teacher / Educator
RIASEC Type: Social (S)
Description: Teachers educate students at school, college or online platforms. Also includes corporate trainers and EdTech content creators.
Skills Required: Communication, Subject Expertise, Patience, Curriculum Design, Digital Tools
Salary India: Govt school 3-8 LPA, Private school 2-6 LPA, College professor 6-18 LPA, EdTech 5-15 LPA
How to Start: Bachelor's in subject → B.Ed → TET exam for govt → Private schools → or EdTech platforms
Relevant for Kerala Students: Kerala highest literacy state, strong teacher culture, CBSE and ICSE schools growing, Byju's and Unacademy hire from Kerala
