Career: Marketing Manager
RIASEC Type: Enterprising (E)
Description: Marketing Managers plan and execute campaigns to promote products or services across digital and traditional channels.
Skills Required: Digital Marketing, SEO, Social Media, Content Strategy, Analytics, Communication, Branding
Salary India: Entry level 3-6 LPA, Mid level 8-20 LPA, Senior 20-45 LPA
How to Start: Any degree → Digital marketing certification → Internship → Junior marketer → Manager
Relevant for Kerala Students: Every Kerala business needs digital marketing, tourism sector huge, remote digital marketing jobs available globally
